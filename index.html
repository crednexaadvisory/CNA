Who we are

Clear thinking for complex financial decisions.

CredNexa Advisory is a boutique consulting practice based in Mumbai. We work with business owners, boards and finance leaders on the decisions that shape an enterprise — how it plans, how it measures and manages risk, how it governs itself and how it prepares for growth.

Our work is grounded in two decades of experience inside the treasury and risk functions of leading private and international banks. We bring that institutional discipline to organisations that need it but don’t need the overhead of a large firm.
