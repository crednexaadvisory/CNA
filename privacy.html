Privacy policy.

Your confidentiality is fundamental to how we work.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how CredNexa Advisory collects and uses personal data through this website, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us — typically your name, company, email address, phone number, enquiry details and any documents you upload through the contact form.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule consultations;
  • To deliver services you engage us for; and
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for unsolicited marketing.

Service providers

Contact-form submissions are processed by our form provider (Formspree) and delivered to our email. The contact form may be protected from spam by Cloudflare Turnstile, which processes limited technical information (such as IP address and browser signals) to distinguish people from bots. Embedded videos are served by YouTube, which may set its own cookies. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Cookies & analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website (for example, pages visited and approximate location). Analytics cookies are set only if you click Accept on the cookie banner; if you click Decline, Google Analytics is not loaded. Your choice is stored in your browser, and you can change it at any time using Cookie Settings in the footer.

Retention & security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to contact@crednexaadvisory.com.
