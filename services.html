Our services.

Six focused consulting practices — combining institutional-grade expertise with personal, transparent engagement.

Scope of our services. CredNexa Advisory provides business, finance and risk consulting. We are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser or Research Analyst and do not provide investment advice, personal financial planning, portfolio management, or recommendations on securities, mutual funds, PMS, AIFs or other investment products. We are not licensed by IRDAI and do not solicit or sell insurance. If you need investment advice, please consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser.
01

Strategic Finance & Business Planning

Helping owners and boards make better financial decisions about their business through structured planning, budgeting and forecasting.

Covers business (corporate) finance only. It does not include personal financial planning or advice on investing in securities.

  • Business strategy & growth planning
  • Annual budgets & rolling forecasts
  • Cash-flow planning & working capital
  • Capital-structure & funding-need analysis
  • Profitability & unit-economics review
  • Performance dashboards & financial reviews
02

Risk Management & Corporate Governance

Strengthening governance, controls and resilience with frameworks proven inside regulated institutions.

Insurable-risk identification maps business exposures only; we do not recommend, solicit or place specific insurance policies.

  • Enterprise risk management frameworks
  • Risk & control self-assessments
  • Internal financial controls
  • Governance & board-reporting structures
  • Policy & procedure design
  • Insurable-risk identification for businesses
  • Regulatory-readiness reviews
  • Board & audit-committee support
03

Treasury & Market Risk Consulting

Practitioner-designed treasury and market-risk frameworks for corporates, banks and NBFCs.

Framework, policy and methodology consulting. We do not provide trading, dealing or investment recommendations.

  • Treasury policy & limit frameworks
  • Market-risk measurement (VaR, sensitivities)
  • Counterparty credit risk & SA-CCR
  • ALM & liquidity-risk frameworks
  • Risk-system requirements & integration
  • Model & process reviews
04

Business Operations Consulting

Streamlining operations so strategy turns into measurable, sustainable performance.

  • Business-process improvement
  • Operational efficiency
  • Cost optimisation
  • Business transformation
  • Management reporting & MIS
  • KPI design & performance metrics
  • Workflow optimisation
  • Strategic decision support
05

Transaction Support

Preparing businesses for important financial transactions with robust analysis and documentation.

We do not act as merchant banker, broker or placement agent, and do not arrange or solicit investment. Statutory valuations, where required by law, are carried out by appropriately registered valuers.

  • Financial due diligence support
  • Financial modelling & business plans
  • Investment-proposal evaluation for businesses
  • Investor-readiness & information memoranda
  • Restructuring analysis
  • Transaction documentation support
06

Corporate Training & Financial Literacy

Building capability inside finance and risk teams — and general financial awareness for employees and communities.

Literacy sessions are generic and educational; they do not include recommendations on specific securities or products.

  • Treasury & market-risk training
  • Basel, SA-CCR & regulatory-capital modules
  • Risk-governance workshops for boards
  • Finance for non-finance managers
  • General financial-literacy sessions
