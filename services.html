Our services.

Nine focused consulting practices — from treasury set-up and Basel compliance to business finance — combining institutional-grade expertise with personal, transparent engagement.

Scope of our services. CredNexa Advisory provides business, finance and risk consulting. We are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser or Research Analyst and do not provide investment advice, personal financial planning, portfolio management, or recommendations on securities, mutual funds, PMS, AIFs or other investment products. We are not licensed by IRDAI and do not solicit or sell insurance. If you need investment advice, please consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser.
01

Treasury Set-up

Comprehensive end-to-end advisory from front-office strategy through mid-office monitoring to operations, risk management and precise accounting integration.

Designing and implementing treasury functions and systems. We do not deal, trade or provide investment recommendations.

  • Treasury strategy, products & organisation design
  • Front-office desk set-up & dealing policies
  • Mid-office: limits, monitoring & risk reporting
  • Back-office operations & settlement workflows
  • Treasury accounting & GL integration
  • Treasury system selection & implementation
  • Policies, SOPs & delegation of authority
  • Go-live readiness & post-implementation review
02

Treasury & Market Risk Consulting

Practitioner-designed treasury and market-risk frameworks for corporates, banks and NBFCs.

Framework, policy and methodology consulting. We do not provide trading, dealing or investment recommendations.

  • Treasury policy & limit frameworks
  • Market-risk measurement (VaR, sensitivities)
  • Counterparty credit risk & SA-CCR
  • ALM & liquidity-risk frameworks
  • Risk-system requirements & integration
  • Model & process reviews
03

RBI / Basel Compliance

Navigating the intricate regulatory landscape of Basel norms and RBI mandates with technical rigour and foresight.

Regulatory consulting for banks, NBFCs and other regulated entities. Final regulatory interpretation rests with the regulator and the entity’s own compliance function.

  • Basel III capital & RWA computation
  • Market-risk capital (standardised approach)
  • Counterparty credit risk & SA-CCR
  • LCR, NSFR & liquidity-risk compliance
  • Interest-rate risk in the banking book
  • RBI master directions & circular impact analysis
  • Regulatory reporting & returns review
  • Gap assessments & remediation roadmaps
04

Ind AS & IFRS Implementation

Expert guidance on AS, Ind AS and IFRS transition, ensuring seamless reporting and alignment with global standards.

Implementation and transition support. We do not provide statutory audit, attestation or certification services.

  • AS to Ind AS / IFRS transition planning
  • Ind AS 109 / IFRS 9 classification & measurement
  • Expected credit loss (ECL) frameworks
  • Hedge accounting design & documentation
  • Fair-value measurement (Ind AS 113)
  • Accounting policies & disclosure templates
  • Financial-reporting process & system changes
  • Training for finance & accounts teams
